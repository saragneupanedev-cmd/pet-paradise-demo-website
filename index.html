Our Story

A lifelong dream, built around one anxious weimaraner

Opening a boarding kennel with quality accommodations, loving care and a truly safe environment was Hazel Richardson's lifelong dream. Since 1999 she has provided superior-quality boarding to Melissa and the surrounding areas.

The property was purpose-built around Hazel's needy (and slightly neurotic) weimaraner, Chester, who stressed so badly in a traditional facility that he lost weight — and his voice. Pet Paradise was designed so that never happens to your dog.

We're a small kennel and we intend to stay that way. We know every guest personally, watch their health and appetite closely, and if someone isn't eating we'll cook chicken and rice or scramble eggs until they do. No friendly dog is turned away — social dogs enjoy group play, shy dogs get one-on-one time.

The manager lives on-site. Every night at 10pm, guests get a bedtime check and one last potty break before lights-out.

The Pet Paradise Difference

The details that keep your pet safe & happy

Good, fresh food

We serve Bil-Jac — a quality food we thaw and keep refrigerated so it's more like fresh food, and our guests love it. Prefer your own? Bring it. If a guest won't eat within two days, we'll find something they will.

Careful flea control

We treat our yards with an environmentally safe insecticide and watch for any scratching. We recommend a vet-approved flea preventive before boarding; any guest with fleas is treated in our grooming room.

Veterinary care

If a guest becomes ill we contact you right away and take them to your own vet when possible. For emergencies we rely on Tri County — our trusted vet since 2002 — and an after-hours emergency clinic in Plano.

Supervised playtimes

All play is supervised. At check-in you decide whether your dog joins group play — and even with your permission, we only socialize guests we're confident are compatible.

Someone always home

Our manager lives on the property, so there's always a familiar person nearby — including a 10pm bedtime check and final potty break every night.

Structured, loving days

Days are structured like a child's daycare — feed times, playtimes, snuggle times, snack times and quiet times — so every guest feels settled, secure and right at home.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What vaccinations does my dog need before boarding?

Dogs must be current on DHLPPC (annually), Rabies (annually) and Bordetella / kennel cough (every six months). Please bring proof from your vet at check-in.

What should I bring for my pet's stay?

You're welcome to bring bedding and toys to make the suite feel like home, plus your pet's own food if you prefer. We serve quality Bil-Jac food and monitor every guest's appetite closely. A boarding agreement is completed at check-in.

Will my dog get to play with other dogs?

Only if you say so. At check-in you decide whether your dog joins supervised group play — and even with your permission, we only socialize guests we're confident are compatible. Dogs who prefer their own space enjoy individual playtime instead.

How does check-in and check-out work?

We charge by the night, just like a hotel. Check-out is 12 noon; after that an additional night is charged. Please drop off and pick up during office hours — Monday–Saturday 8am–12pm & 4pm–6pm, or Sunday 9–11am & 6–7pm.

Do you require deposits or minimum stays?

Weekend bookings (Fri–Sun) have a 2-night minimum and holidays a 3-night minimum. Holiday reservations require a 25% deposit; give 14 days' notice to receive a refund or a credit toward your next visit.

Do you board cats, birds and small pets?

Yes! We have quiet, cozy cat condos, and we welcome birds and small critters — rabbits, guinea pigs, ferrets, hamsters and more. Birds and critters board at $20 per cage, per night; just bring them in their cage. Please call for current cat boarding rates.

What happens if my pet gets sick during their stay?

We contact you right away and take your pet to your own veterinarian when possible. For emergencies we rely on Tri County — our trusted vet since 2002 — and an after-hours emergency clinic in Plano. Our manager also lives on the property, so someone is always nearby.
