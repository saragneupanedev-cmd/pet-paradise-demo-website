Privacy Policy

Last updated:

The short version: we only collect the details you choose to send us so we can respond about boarding your pet. We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing you didn't ask for.

Who we are

Pet Paradise Resort is a family-owned pet boarding, grooming and daycare business located at 1918 Milrany Lane, Melissa, TX 75454. You can reach us at 972-838-2738 or pet_paradise167@yahoo.com. This policy explains how information is handled on this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit the reservation request form, we collect the details you enter — such as your name, phone number, email address, your pet's information and dates. This is used only to respond to your request and arrange your pet's stay.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our host may automatically log standard information such as your browser type, device and approximate region, and the site may use analytics to understand overall traffic. This is aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your reservation request or question and schedule your pet's visit.
  • To keep basic records of bookings and communication.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it.

Third-party services

To provide certain features, this site relies on a few trusted third parties, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Form delivery. Reservation requests are delivered to us through a form-processing service, which transmits your message to our email.
  • Google Maps. The directions map is embedded from Google, which may set cookies and collect usage data under Google's privacy policy.
  • Website analytics. If enabled, an analytics tool helps us measure visits and traffic in aggregate.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for advertising. Embedded services such as Google Maps may set their own cookies. You can control or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

How we protect & retain information

We keep the information you send only as long as needed to serve you and maintain reasonable business records, and we take sensible steps to keep it secure. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Your choices

You may request that we access, correct or delete the personal information you've shared with us — just contact us using the details above and we'll be glad to help.

Children

This website is intended for adults arranging care for their pets and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy
